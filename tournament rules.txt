It's a new tournament format that works as follows:
    There is a tournament rating TGoR that is initially set to the actual GoR of the player
    After each round the GoR is updated according to the GoR-update rules, except with a higher k-factor that usual. This factor is part of the tournament parameters.
    rounds are paired according to the current tournament GoR difference, but two players will not be matched twice against each other in the tournament. And you can define a pairing-penalty for players of the same club, so that the algorithm preferrably pairs players that are not in the same club
    Tournament winner is the player with the highest Tournament-rating.
    Rounds can be played with any handicap, but full handicap is default
